Where this comes from

Rates are the negotiated amounts each payer publishes under the federal Transparency in Coverage rule, for physical-therapy billing codes, for PT providers whose NPPES practice address is in the Twin Cities zip areas listed above. Individual therapists are rolled up to their clinic before any median is taken.

A clinic that is not here either has no rate in these payers' files or is not in the NPPES PT list for these zips. Nothing here is advice; it is the payers' own numbers, side by side.
